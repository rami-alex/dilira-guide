Impornat Info

Reception Hours

Reception services are available
Monday to Saturday, from 09:00 am to 14:00 pm. The reception is located on your right hand as you enter the main gate.

Keys & Access

We have provided you with one set of keys, a card and a controller. The controller is to open the main gate so you can enter our property. With the key, you can open the door of your villa, and you have to place the card at the point with the sign next to the door so you can have electricity.

Wi-Fi

Network: DILIRA VILLAS

When you connect, a pop-up page will appear.
You can find username and password behind the entrance door.

Check in/out Time

Check-in time is at 16:00 PM.
Check-out time is at 11:00 AM

Early check-in and late check-out are available if needed, upon request, and only when there are no other guests checking in or out on the same day.

Cleaning Service & Sheets change

Every 3 or 4 days the bed sheets and the white towels (for bath) are changed and the house is cleaned. Due to the water shortage in Crete, if you're happy to reuse your towels a bit longer, please hang them up so the cleaning staff knows not to replace them. If you don't need for a sheets change, please inform us.

Warm water & Boiler

The water in the villas is heated by solar panels. If water is not warm enough, you can switch on the boiler. You can find the fuses panel next to the main door (for villa Lida and Diana) or in the basement (for villa Rami) and press the button that says "for warm water press here" on the designated boiler fuse switch. Press on and wait for 20min aprox. The boiler switch closes automatically. If water temprature isn't high enough press again and wait.

Cooktop

Kitchen stoves are touch control, so you have to press at specific points to turn them on (video). Also you can press the lock sign to lock your stoves for children safety. If you have any difficulty, please ask for help at the reception.

Drinking Water

In the kitchen, there are two taps. Please use the small tap for drinking water.

Swimming Pool Safety

Please do not let children unattended at the pool and do not jump or dive. We are not responsible for any consequences due to non-compliance with the rules. Please make a shower (there is an outdoor shower beside the pool) before you enter the pool, so you can wash off any sunscreen that you may have used.

Toilet Paper

In Greece, we don't throw toilet paper into the toilet, as it can block the plumbing. Please discard it in the small bathroom bin instead.

Trash Disposal

You can leave your trash outside the villa between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM every day, except Sundays. Please avoid leaving it out in the afternoon, as cats might tear open the bags and spread the rubbish.

Transportation - Taxi services

If you need transport services, we collaborate with a reliable driver. Call Rami-Alex at +30 6949654865 to help you arrange any transfer during your stay.

"Rakadika"

Rakadika are traditional Cretan taverns where people gather to enjoy raki (a strong local spirit), meze (small dishes), music, and great company. Head to the city center and then to Kagiampi street ,where you can find plenty of them.
